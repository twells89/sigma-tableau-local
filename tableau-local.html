Tableau → Sigma Converter — Quick Help

Connect to Tableau

  1. Click ⚡ Auto to fill the form from server-side env vars (TABLEAU_SERVER, TABLEAU_SITE, TABLEAU_PAT_NAME, TABLEAU_PAT_SECRET) — set in start.sh.
  2. Or fill Server URL, Site, PAT Name, PAT Secret manually and click Connect.

Convert a workbook or flow

  1. Toggle 📊 Workbooks or 🔧 Flows, pick one, and click Load Selected. Or drag-drop a .twb/.twbx/.tfl/.tflx file.
  2. Select the data source, the matching Sigma connection, and a join strategy.
  3. Click Convert to Sigma JSON — review output + warnings.

Save to Sigma

  1. Enter Sigma Client ID, Client Secret, and region.
  2. Click Connect to Sigma, pick a workspace + folder, then Save to Sigma.

Known limitations

  • LOD INCLUDE/EXCLUDE not auto-converted — recreate as child elements.
  • Window calcs (LOOKUP, WINDOW_SUM, etc.) flagged but not converted.
  • Multi-connection data blending not supported (each datasource converts independently).
  • Top-N / Bottom-N sets — recreate as filters in Sigma.
